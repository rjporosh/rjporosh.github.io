about

I've spent the last six-plus years writing production code that businesses depend on — most of it in .NET Core / ASP.NET Core, alongside React, Angular, and cross-platform mobile with Xamarin and .NET MAUI. The through-line across every job has been systems that can't afford to fail quietly: payment infrastructure, banking authentication, and e-commerce platforms running at national scale.

At Softrobotics Bangladesh, I built and deployed a payment gateway, ERP, HRM, and accounting platform on a microservices architecture, stress-tested for reliability under load. Earlier, at AllOne SoftTech, I helped ship Bangladesh's first biometric SDK for banking, putting AI/ML-based facial recognition into production at five major banks. Before that, at AjkerDeal / BdJobs, I helped build one of the country's largest e-commerce platforms and shipped its first live-video shopping and live-chat features.

I care more about ownership than titles — understanding the business problem, choosing the right architecture, and staying accountable for what ships. I'm also actively building in AI/ML and automation on the side, which is where a lot of my independent projects live — including a voice-driven assistant inside my library management system and an LLM-powered email triage agent.
